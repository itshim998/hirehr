Welcome to HireHR Services

We believe service determines the success and failure of a business relationship.

Permanent Hiring Solutions

Finding the right candidate for a permanent role can be time-consuming and costly. We source and place candidates who match both the role and your organization's culture.

Contract hiring Solutions

Short-term or long-term projects? We provide contract staffing that helps reduce overtime costs, lower paperwork, and keep resources placed on time.

Recruitment Process Outsourcing

We take on all or part of your recruitment process so you can focus on your core business. Our team works onsite and offshore to handle campus hiring, bulk hiring, and more.

Background Verification Services

We assist employers with verifying employment history and conducting reference checks on prospective new employees.

IT Hiring

We can help you find suitable IT profiles for your internal hiring and for your clients. Whether you need Java or .Net, or newer skills like Cloud, AI and ML, we have ready resources for most IT skills.

ITES Hiring

We can help you find the right candidates for your BPO and KPO divisions. We have expertise in bulk hiring and campus hiring in the ITES recruitment field.

GENERAL Hiring

If you need help hiring non-IT profiles, we can help you there too. We assist with non-IT hiring from support staff to management level (CEO/Director).

Who we are ?

Who we are ?

HireHR Services is a manpower hiring service provider headquartered in Kolkata, West Bengal. We do not claim to be the best, but we always try to give our clients the best service we can. We help employers source the best possible manpower to fulfill their hiring needs, and job seekers can reach out to us for career advice and interview guidance.
